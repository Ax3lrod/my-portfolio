# Product Guidelines: Cyberpunk Tech Portfolio

## Tone of Voice: Guerilla / Punk
- **Energetic & Raw:** Technical descriptions should feel like a "debrief" from a field operation.
- **Unconventional:** Use bold language that emphasizes subverting standard web norms and "hacking" together high-performance solutions.
- **Direct:** Avoid corporate jargon; speak with the clarity of someone who knows their tools inside and out.

## Visual Hierarchy: Balanced Chaos
- **HUD Density:** Information is presented in a dense, non-linear layout inspired by futuristic terminal interfaces.
- **Equal Weight:** 3D assets and technical data share the screen; neither is sacrificed for the other.
- **Exploratory Navigation:** Encourage the user (recruiter) to "interact" with the UI to reveal deeper layers of data.

## Motion & Interactivity
- **Ambient & High Intensity:** The interface should feel "alive" with subtle breathing animations and scanlines, punctuated by high-intensity glitch effects on interaction.
- **Responsive Energy:** Hover states and page transitions should be snappy and impactful, signaling a high-performance system.

## Brand Messaging: The Architect
- **Engineering Core:** Position the portfolio as the work of an "Architect" who engineers complex, high-performance systems.
- **Edge-Case Mastery:** Highlight projects that push the boundaries of what is possible in the browser (3D, motion, performance).

## UI/UX Principles
- **No Generic Components:** Every UI element (buttons, frames, inputs) must be custom-styled, avoiding standard browser or library defaults.
- **System Artifacts:** Intentional use of scanlines, digital grain, and slight CRT distortion to maintain the "Retro-Tech" immersion.
- **Neon-on-Dark:** Strict high-contrast palette to ensure "Cyberpunk" authenticity.
